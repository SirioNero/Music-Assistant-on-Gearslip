New: “Make Xonos your Music Assistant server”. No Music Assistant at home? From Settings, Xonos downloads Music Assistant (about 70 MB, only if you say yes) and runs it on the phone itself, just for itself and for myCar: add the phone’s music, radio, podcasts and streaming services. The download comes from this page, and Xonos checks that it’s signed like Xonos. 64-bit phones only.
