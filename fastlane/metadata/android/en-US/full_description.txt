Xonos turns your phone into a private Music Assistant player and brings your Music Assistant library to Gearslip's music player in the car, including the split screen next to the map. The music plays on the phone itself, so it reaches the car over Bluetooth (or Gearslip's USB audio), never your speakers at home.

You need your own Music Assistant server, version 2.7.5 or later (adding services from the app needs 2.10). Whatever Music Assistant can play is in Xonos: your own files and servers (Jellyfin, Plex, Subsonic, Navidrome), podcasts, radio and the services you connect to Music Assistant.

<b>On the phone</b>
<ul>
<li>Home with what you played recently and Music Assistant's recommendations</li>
<li>Search across the library and every service, while you type</li>
<li>The whole library: playlists, albums, artists, tracks, podcasts, audiobooks, radio, genres</li>
<li>A player with synced lyrics and the queue</li>
<li>Play on: send the music to the speakers Music Assistant knows (Chromecast, AirPlay, Sonos, DLNA…) and bring it back to the phone, from the same point</li>
<li>Finds Music Assistant on your network and signs in with your username and password</li>
<li>Music Assistant administrators can add, set up and remove services from the app</li>
</ul>

<b>In the car, through Gearslip</b>
<ul>
<li>The full menu and search, in the language you chose</li>
<li>Calls and navigation prompts pause or lower the music</li>
<li>Short losses of signal (a tunnel) are covered by the audio already received</li>
</ul>

Available in English, Italiano, Español, Deutsch, Français, Português, Ελληνικά, Русский and 简体中文.

Xonos is an independent project, not affiliated with Music Assistant or Gearslip.
